Telegram bot for a beauty salon

Most clients decide whether to book by chatting. The bot answers questions about services and prices and collects booking requests while your staff are busy.

What the bot does

  • Answers about services, prices and masters
  • Collects booking requests: name, service, preferred time
  • Reminders about visits sent to subscribers
  • Answers to voice messages from clients
  • Works around the clock, even when the salon is closed

Questions and answers

Does the bot book clients automatically?

It collects the request and passes it to you. Your administrator confirms the booking, so you stay in control.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days, depending on how much information about services you provide.
