AI Telegram bot for a school or tutor

Students need help at any time, and a tutor cannot answer around the clock. The bot explains topics in simple words and helps work through a task without doing it for the student.

What the bot does

  • Explanations of topics in simple language
  • Help with tasks from photos
  • Help with code and programming, with examples
  • Voice questions: students can speak instead of typing
  • Conversation memory, so the bot remembers what was already covered

Questions and answers

Will the bot give ready answers to homework?

It can be set up to hint and explain the steps instead. The rules are set for your school or tutoring style.

Can the topics be limited?

Yes. The bot can be set to answer only in the subject you teach.
