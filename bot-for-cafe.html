Telegram bot for a café or restaurant

Guests ask about the menu, opening hours and bookings all the time. The bot answers these questions and takes table requests.

What the bot does

  • Menu, prices and opening hours in the answers
  • Table booking requests: name, date, time, number of guests
  • Answers to photos of dishes and voice messages
  • Promotions and new dishes sent to subscribers
  • Works around the clock

Questions and answers

Does the bot confirm bookings?

No. It collects the request and passes it to your staff, who confirm the booking.

Can I change the menu later?

Yes. The menu and prices can be updated on request.
