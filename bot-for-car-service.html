Telegram bot for a car service

Clients ask how much an oil change costs and when you have time. The bot answers from your price list and collects booking requests while the mechanics are busy.

What the bot does

  • Price list and approximate prices in the answers
  • Booking requests: car, service, preferred time
  • Photo understanding: the client sends a photo of a fault and the bot asks the right questions
  • Answers to voice messages from clients
  • Service reminders sent to subscribers

Questions and answers

Can the bot give an exact repair price?

It quotes prices from your price list and says that the final cost is set by the mechanic after inspection.

Can the bot remind clients about service dates?

Yes, reminders can be sent to clients who agreed to receive them.
